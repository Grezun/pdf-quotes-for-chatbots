Privacy Policy — PDF Quotes for Chatbots

Last updated: 10 September 2026

PDF Quotes for Chatbots ("the App") is a SendPulse App Directory application that generates PDF price quotes from data collected by your chatbot. This policy describes what data the App stores and how it is handled.

Data we store

  • Business settings you enter on the settings page: company name, contact details, logo, brand color, currency, and your list of services and prices.
  • Quote records created when your chatbot requests a quote: the customer name and service details your bot sends, the calculated price, and the generated PDF document.
  • Account linkage: identifiers provided by SendPulse when you install the App, used solely to connect your SendPulse account to your settings.

How data is used

Data is used only to generate quote PDFs and show them to your customers via download links. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties beyond the hosting infrastructure below.

Storage & security

  • Data is stored on Supabase (hosted in the EU, Frankfurt region).
  • Generated PDFs and logos are stored in private storage. They are accessible only through expiring signed links (quote links expire after 30 days).
  • API access requires your private API key; settings access requires your private settings link.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the App from your SendPulse account deactivates your configuration and permanently deletes your stored PDFs and logo. To request full deletion of remaining records, contact us at the address below.
